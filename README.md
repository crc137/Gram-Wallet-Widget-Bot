## What it does

- Home-screen widget with your wallet card
- GRAM / TON balance and USD value
- Card look with Telegram-style gifts, background, and pattern
- Receive QR and copy address
- Payment history, tokens, and NFTs
- Optional local transaction notifications
- In-app update banner when a new APK is out
- Supporter badge for people who donated (optional)

View-only: public address only. No transaction signing, no seed phrases, no private keys.

Not affiliated with Telegram or the TON Foundation.
